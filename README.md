# Literature Review

The current study investigates the effectiveness of different route-planning approaches for visitors to the Prigan farm, focusing on the user experience and overall satisfaction with their visit. A fundamental aspect of this research lies in understanding the balance between user autonomy and AI-assisted planning in navigation systems. 

Previous studies have identified critical advantages and disadvantages associated with user-generated routes versus AI-generated suggestions. According to studies by Harms et al. (2025), allowing users to create their own routes fosters a higher sense of control and autonomy, enhancing their spatial understanding and trust in the navigation process. Conversely, the research highlights that the cognitive load associated with making numerous decisions can lead to decision fatigue, negatively impacting the user experience (Harms et al., 2025).

In contrast, AI-generated route suggestions streamline the navigation process, especially for first-time visitors (Wang et al., 2024). However, research by Chen and Zhang (2023) notes that users often exhibit skepticism towards AI recommendations due to perceived opacity in AI decision-making. A lack of trust in AI-generated routes can result in users modifying these suggestions, seeking more tangible control over their experience (Chen & Zhang, 2023).

The research conducted by Thompson et al. (2026) further elaborates that users often prefer itineraries designed by humans or through manual control rather than fully relying on AI. This finding is particularly relevant to the Prigan context, where visitors seek meaningful engagement with the farm's various attractions, including hot pepper varieties. Studies show that when users understand the rationale behind AI suggestions, their trust and overall satisfaction with the navigation experience tend to increase (Thompson et al., 2026).

Designing a responsive web interface, such as the Prigan Guide, may enhance user experience by integrating human-centric design principles with AI suggestions. By providing an explainable AI route recommendation, the guide can potentially reduce cognitive load and improve user confidence in navigation (Robinson & Patel, 2025). 

In summary, the current research will build on the existing body of literature, focusing on how the dual approach of user route creation and AI assistance can lead to improved visitor experience at Prigan. The ultimate aim is to determine how an explainable AI navigation system might better facilitate planning and following a visit route compared to traditional manual methods.
